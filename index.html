Bioinformatics Methods & Formulas

All calculations run client-side in O(n) time with zero server dependencies.

Validation & Cleaning

FASTA headers (>...), whitespace, line breaks, and digits are stripped and letters uppercased. Only the 20 standard amino acids (ACDEFGHIKLMNPQRSTVWY) enter calculations. Ambiguous or non-standard characters (B, Z, X, U, O, J, *, -, etc.) are reported with counts and excluded.

Molecular Weight & Isoelectric Point

  • MW (Da): Sum of average residue masses + 18.01524 Da (H2O).
  • pI & Net Charge: Henderson-Hasselbalch bisection ([0, 14]) using EMBOSS pKa values (N-term 8.6, C-term 3.6, K 10.8, R 12.5, H 6.5, D 3.9, E 4.1, C 8.5, Y 10.1).
  • Note: pI may differ slightly from ExPASy ProtParam, which uses Bjellqvist's residue-specific pKa set.

Hydropathy, Aliphatic Index & 3D

  • GRAVY: Mean Kyte-Doolittle (1982) hydropathy across all valid residues.
  • Hydrophobicity Profile: Sliding window (5–21, default 9); downsampled to ≤2,000 peak-preserving min/max points for long sequences.
  • Aliphatic Index: Ikai (1980): X(A) + 2.9·X(V) + 3.9·(X(I) + X(L)).
  • Instability Index: Omitted rather than approximated (full 400-entry Guruprasad 1990 DIWV matrix not loaded).
